GOES-19 Ottawa Meshcore Bot beta

Live local weather for the Ottawa mesh: Mention the bot with a place name and it answers with readings from the nearest station. Everything runs over radio, from the satellite to the mesh, with no internet involved.

Stations across the Ottawa Valley send their readings on UHF (401.8 MHz) to GOES-19, NOAA's geostationary weather satellite at 75.2° W, which relays them back down in its HRIT broadcast on 1694.1 MHz.

See the manual for how to use it.

Manual

GOES19 Meshcore Bot

Name

GOES19 – Weather readings from stations across the Ottawa Valley, received via GOES-19's downlink.

Synopsis

@[GOES19] !temp | !humidity | !wind | !pressure | !rain1 | !rain24 location
@[GOES19] !station | !info id
@[GOES19] !find location
@[GOES19] !type emoji
@[GOES19] !status
@[GOES19] !stats
@[GOES19] !suggestion text
@[GOES19] !ping
@[GOES19] !help [category]

Description

The bot listens on the #weather and #bots channels and answers a message that mentions it, @[GOES19], and carries a command. The mention can be anywhere in the message. A message without the mention gets no reply.

A location is a city, town, village or neighbourhood inside the YOW boundary. Case, accents and punctuation don't matter, and small typos are forgiven. An id is a station's 8-character ID from the list above.

Commands

!temp location
🌡️ Air temperature.
!humidity location
💧 Relative humidity.
!wind location
🎐 Wind speed in km/h, with the direction it blows from when the station reports it.
!pressure location
⚖️ Atmospheric pressure in hPa.
!rain1 location
☔ Rain in the last hour, in mm.
!rain24 location
☔ Rain in the last 24 hours, in mm.

For these six commands, the bot picks the station closest to the location that has reported that sensor in the last 3 hours. The reply says where that station is (📍) and how old the reading is (⏱️).

!station id
Every sensor of that station's latest message, rain as the total of the last hour and of the last 24 hours (☔), then its name (📋) and the age of the message. Readings that don't fit in one message are left out and counted (+3).
!info id
What the station is: name (📋), nearest settlement (📍), coordinates (🗺️), agency (🏢) and one emoji per kind of sensor.
!find location
The IDs of the 3 stations nearest the location, with their distance.
!type emoji
What one emoji of the replies stands for, as in the emoji table.
!status
Whether the GOES-19 downlink is being decoded (🛰️ OK, or ERR when nothing has come in for 5 minutes), the frequency the receiver is tuned to (📻), the signal-to-noise ratio of the signal (📶), whether the Viterbi decoder (🧮) and the deframer (🎞️) are in sync (SYNCED or NOSYNC) and how long ago the newest station message was received (📡).
!stats
Bot statistics: stations, sensor types, boundary area, commands, version and last update.
!suggestion text
Sends a suggestion for the bot to its operator, with your name and the channel.
!ping
🏓 Pong!, with how many hops your message took to reach the bot and the repeaters it came through.
!help [category]
Without a category, the list of categories. With one, its commands:
!help ping
!ping.
!help sensors
!temp, !humidity, !wind, !pressure, !rain1, !rain24, !type.
!help stations
!station, !info, !find.
!help utils
!status, !stats, !suggestion.
!bots
On #bots, without a mention: the bot introduces itself.

Diagnostics

unknown location: name
No place of that name inside the YOW boundary.
no recent sensor near place
No station reported that sensor in the last 3 hours.
unknown station: id
Not the ID of a station in the list.
no readings from id
No reading has been received from the station.
unknown emoji: emoji
Not an emoji of the replies; the emoji table lists them.
usage: …
The command needs a location, an ID, an emoji or a text.
Could not save your suggestion, try again later.
The suggestion could not be saved; send it again later.
Under maintenance, try again later.
The bot is switched to maintenance; every command gets this reply.
